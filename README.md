### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Master’s student in Computer Science** at **Binghamton University**, currently working as a **Graduate Research Assistant** in the Marine Robotics Lab. My focus lies at the intersection of **Generative AI, MLOps, and Embedded Systems**.

* 🔭 I’m currently building **End-to-End Automation for AUVs** using Reinforcement Learning.
* 💡 I specialize in **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **LLM Optimization (Quantization/Pruning)**, and **Scalable ML Pipelines**.
* 🌱 I’m deeply interested in **AI Ethics** and **Bias Mitigation** in Large Language Models.
* ⚡ **Fun Fact:** I once trained a GAN to generate abstract art pieces!
